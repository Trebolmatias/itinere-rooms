FAQ

Before you arrive.

Quick answers to common questions. For anything specific, message us directly.

Is private parking available?

Yes. We have private parking in the city centre, with limited spaces and advance reservation required. Because traffic is restricted in the centre, please contact us before arrival for access instructions.

What time is check-in?

Check-in starts at 2:00 pm. If you need to arrive late, self check-in can be arranged. Check-out is at 12:00 noon.

Can I leave my luggage before or after my stay?

Yes, luggage storage can usually be arranged so you can enjoy more of the day. Please coordinate it with us in advance.

Do all rooms have a private bathroom?

We offer rooms with private bathrooms as well as a shared-bathroom option. Please check the room name carefully before booking.

Is there a kitchen?

There is a small shared kitchen with a microwave, fridge, coffee maker, sink and basic utensils—ideal for breakfast and light meals.

How do I get here from the airport?

The airport bus connects with central Granada and stops along Gran Vía. We can also advise you about taxis or private transfers.
